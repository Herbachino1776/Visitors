# VISITORS — Future Lanes Parking Lot

This file preserves strong ideas without forcing them into the first prototype.

## Larger tomb design

The final game should use one permanent, interconnected tomb that unfolds over time.

Future features:
- sealed chambers opening after certain visitors
- shortcuts that make corpse dragging smarter
- vertical shafts
- hidden ritual rooms
- servant dens
- deeper engine chambers
- old Egyptian areas giving way to older Anunnaki/Atlantean/Visitor architecture
- damaged rooms after failed waves
- restored or corrupted rooms after processing strong corpses

## Stronger visitor escalation

Later visitors should feel like legendary invaders, not stat bumps.

Possible lanes:
- armored kings
- holy orders
- beast masters
- necromancers
- minotaurs
- Atlantean claimants
- desert warlords
- corpse-saints
- giant hunters
- Anunnaki-related retrieval agents
- final Visitor owner

Example high-wave concept:
A Nordic king in whale-bone rune armor who can command dead giants and a giant Cerberus-like beast.

Do not build this in v0. Preserve it as proof of scale.

## More processing stations

Future stations:
- Organ Altar
- Offering Pit
- Soul Engine
- Servant Pit
- Spell Wound Table
- Relic Disassembler
- Engine Interface

Each station should be physical. No abstract reward screens unless they are temporary development UI.

## Organ system

Future organs can unlock specific spells or upgrades:
- heart: health / endurance
- eye: reveal / detection
- tongue: silence / anti-magic
- hand: grip / weapon handling
- lung: breath / poison / stamina
- brain: forbidden knowledge / spell unlock

Keep the mechanic legible. Do not turn it into spreadsheet clutter.

## Servants

The tomb should eventually contain weak servants who try to slow visitors.

Possibilities:
- mummies
- ghouls
- jackal-things
- priest remnants
- stone servitors
- failed guardians

Servants should not replace direct combat. They make the visitor feel dangerous by dying in the halls before the Guardian arrives.

## Failure consequences

If the Guardian loses:
- he is thrown back one wave
- previous visitor damage may remain
- a room may be plundered
- resources may be lost
- a corpse-processing opportunity may be missed

Keep failure painful but not run-ending.

## Final reveal

The final invader may be an original Visitor returning for the artifact.

This should not feel like a normal boss. It should feel like the owner entering a ruined museum, finding savages worshiping its property, and deciding the matter is over.
