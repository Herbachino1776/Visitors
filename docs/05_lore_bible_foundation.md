# VISITORS — Lore Bible Foundation

## Working title

**VISITORS**

## Core premise

A tomb was built around an artifact linked to the Visitors, an ancient precursor race. The artifact was later held in extreme regard by the Anunnaki, the way humanity regards pyramids, Göbekli Tepe, sacred relics, museum antiquities, and impossible ruins.

The artifact is not merely powerful. It is culturally sacred to beings far older than humanity.

Humans, gods, kings, priests, beasts, and warlords misunderstand it. They call it treasure, a god-heart, a resurrection engine, a star relic, or a key to dominion. None of those names are fully correct.

## The Guardian

The Guardian was once part of an Egyptian priesthood selected by the Anunnaki to protect the artifact.

This was not a normal priesthood. It was a human interface layer for something older. The priests performed rituals they only partly understood. Their bodies, temples, tools, and language were gradually modified by the duty.

The Guardian is what remains.

He is not free. Death does not release him. Failure throws him backward into the curse-cycle.

## The curse-cycle

Every fictional age, roughly 600 years apart, new visitors come.

If the Guardian wins, the tomb survives and grows stronger.

If the Guardian loses, he is set back. A visitor plunders deeper, damages the tomb, and the Guardian must re-fight prior threats to restore what was lost.

The curse is not only punishment. It is the tomb’s defense mechanism.

## The visitors

Lowercase visitors:
- thieves
- sellswords
- princes
- mages
- kings
- beasts
- cultists
- warlords
- saints
- conquerors
- entities

Uppercase Visitors:
- original owners or ancient precursor race tied to the artifact

The title should remain clean and ambiguous as long as possible.

## The artifact

The artifact should not be fully explained early.

It may be called different things by different cultures:
- the Engine
- the Visitor Core
- the First Relic
- the Black Instrument
- the Museum God
- the Unreturned Object

The strongest long-term lane:
The artifact was not made by the Anunnaki. They found it, revered it, preserved it, or stole it. To the Visitors, it may simply be a missing object.

## Final direction

The final invader is not a normal hero.

The original owner returns.

This being does not enter like a thief. It enters like someone reclaiming property. That should destabilize everything the Guardian believes about duty, theft, holiness, and defense.

## Tone references, abstracted

The game can draw tonal inspiration from:
- hostile first-person dungeon crawling
- ancient mythic adventure
- medieval cosmic horror
- dark high fantasy
- ancient alien dread
- ritual body horror
- cryptic environmental lore

Do not copy specific names, enemies, maps, items, or lore from existing games or films.
